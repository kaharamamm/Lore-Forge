Kahara came to the foot of The Snowcrown with a caravan of The Sunsea. When the First Rockfall buried the camp in the night, she was one of the seven who woke in time. Two years later she melted down the camp's pots and cast The Hero's Bell, and hung it where every house could hear it. Round its rim she wrote: I ring for the hero who rings me.

Her statue stands in the middle of the Seven in front of The Hall of Heroes, holding a stone bell. She would have hated it.
