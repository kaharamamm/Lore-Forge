Ayla's father lights the lamps of The Hall of Heroes, and she has grown up in its attics among the dust and the pigeons. She knows every handhold on the back of the bell tower, though she had never been inside the belfry. She does not enter the Hero Trials: walls, she says, are better when nobody is watching.
