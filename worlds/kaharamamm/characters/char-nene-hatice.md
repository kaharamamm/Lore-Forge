Hatice has sold snow-cream at The Long Paddle in The Copper Market since she was nineteen. In the Great Rockfall of 466, when she was fifteen, she was the one who ran to the tower and rang The Hero's Bell. The Herald of those days told the city it had rung by itself, patted her on the head, and nobody ever asked her again.

She has never thought much of the Hero Trials.
