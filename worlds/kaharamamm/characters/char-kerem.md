Kerem lives above The Long Paddle with his grandmother Nene Hatice, while his parents drive the snow-cream caravans across The Sunsea to Camelamamm. He has practised for the Hero Trials since he was eight, lifting snow pails and climbing the pepper terraces.

He is not strong, and he is not fast, and he is the only person in Kaharamamm who goes up to The Mirror Tarn every morning before dawn. That turns out to matter more.
