Kaharamamm is called the City of Heroes, and it likes being called that. In the old caravan tongue a name ending in -amamm means the place where someone stayed: Camelamamm, where the camels stayed; Kaharamamm, where Kahara stayed. Its caravans carry snow-cream in straw-packed ice-jars south across The Sunsea, and bring back tea.
