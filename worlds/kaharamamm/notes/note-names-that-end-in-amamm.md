In the old caravan tongue of The Sunsea, -amamm means the place where someone stayed. Camelamamm is where the camels stayed. Kaharamamm is where Kahara stayed, after the First Rockfall, when everybody else would have moved on.

Mountain folk also say that Kaharamamm sounds like the word for hero. The Heralds of the Hall like that version better.
