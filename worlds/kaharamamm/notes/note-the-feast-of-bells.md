The Feast of Bells is held on the first three days of Highheat, when the snow begins to melt. Every street is hung with copper bells.

From 470 to 527 its second day was the Hero Trials, held in The Square of Bells: the Stone, the Wall and the Stair. The champion walked through the arch beneath The Hero's Bell, and the whole city waited for it to ring. It never did. The third night was the Champion's Feast.

Since 527 there are no Trials. On the second day the whole city climbs to The Ridge of the Seven instead, so that everyone remembers the way.
