Snow-cream is made from fresh mountain snow that nobody has stepped on, goat's milk, mountain honey, orchid flour and a pinch of tear-resin. It is beaten in a cold copper tub with a long paddle for the best part of an hour, until it is thick and glossy and can be pulled out into a rope as long as your arm without breaking.

The makers of The Paddle Guild tease their customers: they hold out the cone, whip the cream away on the paddle, give it back, take it again, until everybody laughs. Nene Hatice can stretch a scoop across the whole width of The Copper Market.
